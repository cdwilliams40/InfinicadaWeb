The Ritual

How the magic works

Three steps between you and a distraction-free session.

01

Enchant

Create an Enchantment — a blocklist of apps and websites. Choose Banish mode to block specific apps, or Shield mode to allow only the ones you need.

02

Cast

Start your focus spell with a tap of an NFC talisman, a QR code scan, a Quick Spell preset, or a scheduled Ritual. Set a duration, allow breaks — then cast.

03

Dispel

When the session ends, the spell lifts on its own. With Talisman Lock enabled, only a tap of your talisman can dispel it early — no willpower required.

✦ Deeper magic, for when you need it

  • Talisman Lock — require an NFC tap or QR scan to end a session early, so quitting takes more than a swipe.
  • Configurable breaks — allow a set number of timed breaks per session, with early-end when you're ready to resume.
  • Emergency Stop — one escape hatch for true emergencies, on a weeks-long cooldown so it can't become a habit.
  • Notification silencing — mute notifications and enable Do Not Disturb while focusing, restored during breaks.
  • Gentle redirection — open a blocked app and you're calmly guided back to your task, not shamed.
  • Survives reboots — active sessions and scheduled rituals pick right back up after a device restart.
  • Auto-breaks (Pomodoro) — start a break automatically after each focused stretch, tuned anywhere from 5 to 60 minutes.
  • Hidden stop button — optionally remove the stop button entirely, so a session can't be ended on impulse.
  • Pact circles — bind one pact to a whole enchantment: every app in it is sealed with the same settings, new installs included.
  • Healthier substitutes — a pact can offer "Open Kindle instead", usable immediately while the real choice waits out the pause.
Maximum Enforcement

Summon the Warden

For when an app-level block isn't enough: make Focus Pocus the device owner, and Android itself enforces the spell.

  • 🛡️

    Blocking the OS enforces

    During a focus session, blocked apps are suspended at the system level — launcher icons grey out and Android refuses to open them. No overlay race, no split-second flash of your feed.

  • ⛓️

    Uninstall-proof willpower

    While Warden Mode is active, Focus Pocus cannot be uninstalled — so a 1 a.m. moment of weakness can't undo what your better self set up.

  • 🕊️

    Transparent, reversible, no root

    Built on Android's standard device-owner role and used for exactly two things: suspending blocked apps and blocking uninstall. Remove it from Settings anytime — just never mid-session.

Read the Warden Mode guide The technicalities
the summoning — one time only
$ adb shell dpm set-device-owner \ com.infinicada.focuspocus/.FocusDeviceAdminReceiver Success: Device owner set to package com.infinicada.focuspocus # icons grey out the moment you cast ✨

One command from a computer with adb, and the Warden holds the keys. The same steps are shown in-app under Settings → Warden Mode, with tap-to-copy commands.

Privacy

Effective Date: January 30, 2026

Your data stays on your device.

Focus Pocus is built with a privacy-first philosophy. All app activity, blocking configurations, and usage stats are stored locally and never uploaded.

  • No Personal Data: No name, email, account, or personal information is ever collected.
  • No Advertising ID: The advertising identifier is explicitly removed — there is no cross-app tracking or ad targeting.
  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account to use Focus Pocus.
  • Permissions Stay Local: The app requires several Android permissions to power its features — all data stays on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Anonymous Analytics (Optional): The app sends anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics to help fix bugs — no personal data is ever included. Sharing is on by default, and you can turn it off during onboarding or anytime in Settings.
